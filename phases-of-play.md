<p class="diagram-caption">The game cycles through all four, over and over, every match.</p>

**In Possession** — we have the ball. Beat the next line.

**Transition to Defence** — we've just lost it. First to react wins it back.

**Out of Possession** — settled without the ball. Squeeze the middle, show them wide.

**Transition to Attack** — we've just won it. Look up before you settle.

Whichever one you're in, the same question applies: which Space are you in, and what's your job right now? See [On-Pitch Prompts]({{ '/on-pitch-prompts/' | relative_url }}) for the cue in each.
