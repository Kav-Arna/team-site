<p class="diagram-caption">11, 10 and 7 hold the half-spaces and the middle.</p>

The pitch splits into five vertical lanes — wide, half-space, central, half-space, wide. We hold the middle three of those pretty much always. The central lane isn't the same width as the half-spaces either side of it — it's wider, because it runs six-yard box to six-yard box, while each half-space only runs from the six-yard box out to the edge of the penalty box.

3 lanes always, a 4th on the ball side when it's on — never both wide lanes at once.
