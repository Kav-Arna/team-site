<p class="diagram-caption">Back four, double pivot, a narrow front three either side of the central lane, one striker.</p>

A back four, a double pivot in front of them, three attacking midfielders narrow through the half-spaces and central lane, and one striker leading the line. The faint vertical lines mark the pitch lanes (see [Pitch Lanes]({{ '/pitch-lanes/' | relative_url }})) — the front three's whole job is staying connected inside them, not spreading across the full width of the pitch.
