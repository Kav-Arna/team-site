<p class="diagram-caption">Space 1, 2 and 3 — the rings expand outward from the ball, not from fixed pitch zones.</p>

**Space 1** is you and your direct opponent — the tight duel right around the ball.

**Space 2** is the next layer out — the players close enough to support the ball straight away.

**Space 3** is everyone else. Not near the ball doesn't mean not involved — you're still dragging a marker, holding a line, or opening a lane for later.

The one question that runs through everything: **which Space are you in, and what's your job right now?**
