Unlike a corner, a throw-in doesn't get a fixed, rehearsed pattern. It resets play into a normal picture — the same Space 1/2/3 read you'd make anywhere else on the pitch.

<div class="qbox">
<p>Show before it's thrown, not after.</p>
</div>

<span class="space-tag">Space 1</span> The nearest receiver shows early — moving before the ball arrives, not waiting on it.

<span class="space-tag">Space 2</span> The next player offers a return angle or an out-ball up the line.

<span class="space-tag">Space 3</span> Everyone else keeps the same shape as open play — three lanes, don't bunch toward the ball.
