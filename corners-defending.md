## Central Cover

**#9** — central, midway between the halfway line and the D. Reacts to clearances.

**#10** — edge of the box. Knockdowns and poorly struck clearances.

## Short Corner Cover

The ball-side attacking midfielder covers the short corner or a poorly hit delivery.

## Everyone Else

Man-for-man: goal-side and ball-side of your attacker.

## Spare Players

Double up on their biggest attacking threat.
