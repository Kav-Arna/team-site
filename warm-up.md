The keeper warms up separately throughout the Gates Game section.

## FIFA 11+ (Modified) — 12 min

Continuous shuttle, a 20–25m channel. Pulse-raiser runs and mobility drills alternated, not blocked — keeps intensity building rather than stop-start.

## Gates Game — 8 min

Continuous possession-to-transition. Score by dribbling through a gate under control, or a first-time pass through one to a runner. Live on turnover — no whistle, no reset.

15 outfield players, no keeper. Run two grids side by side, not one crowded grid — keeps space and touches honest.

## Line-Up & Team Talk — 5 min

Bring both grids in together, keeper joins from goal. This is when the [One Thing]({{ '/matchday/' | relative_url }}) for the week gets delivered — to a group that's just physically lived the Space 1/2/3 language in the Gates Game.
