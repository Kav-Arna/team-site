## FIFA 11+ (Modified) — 12 min

Continuous shuttle, a 20–25m channel. Running and mobility drills are alternated, not blocked, so intensity keeps building rather than stopping and starting.

1. Jog
2. Heel flicks
3. Leg swings
4. Hip out
5. Hip in
6. Abductor stretch (walking)
7. Circling
8. Walking deadlifts
9. Bounding
10. Two forwards, one back
11. Jog out, 70% sprint back

<div class="note">
This order is a suggested integration, not a fixed prescription — the original FIFA 11+ runs stay in sequence, with the mobility drills slotted in wherever the running channel gives a natural walk-back. Adjust to whatever the squad already has grooved in; consistency week to week matters more than this exact order.
</div>

## Gates Game — 8 min

Continuous possession-to-transition. Score by dribbling through a gate under control, or a first-time pass through one to a runner. Live on turnover — no whistle, no reset.

**15 outfield players, no keeper** (keeper warms up separately). Run **two grids side by side**, not one crowded grid — it keeps space and touches honest.

- **Grid A:** 3v3 + 2 floaters (8 players)
- **Grid B:** 3v3 + 1 floater (7 players)

At the **4-minute mark**, call "swap" — floaters trade places so Grid B gets 2 and Grid A drops to 1. One swap, everyone gets even floater time across the 8 minutes. Floaters always play for whichever team in their grid has the ball. Two gates per end line, set in the half-space lanes.

**Coach cues from between the two grids — cues, don't stop play:**

| Moment | Cue |
|---|---|
| Player dribbling at a defender | "Which Space are you in?" / "Pin or bait!" |
| Defender jumps in | "2v1 — pass!" |
| Defender holds off | "Drive it!" |
| Ball turned over | "First to react wins it back!" |
| Team just won it | "Look up!" |
| Defending shape stretching | "Squeeze the middle!" |

## Line-Up & Team Talk — 5 min

- Bring both grids in together, keeper joins from goal.
- [One Thing]({{ '/matchday/' | relative_url }}) — today's single message, delivered to a group that's just physically lived the Space 1/2/3 language.
